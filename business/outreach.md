# Outreach templates (English)

Rule: lead with a free result, not a pitch. Never spam; one message per person.

## Where to find launches
- X: search `"just launched"`, `"shipped"`, `#buildinpublic`, `"launching on Product Hunt"` — sort by Latest.
- Product Hunt: today's and upcoming launches without a video.
- Hacker News: "Show HN" posts with a live site.
- Indie Hackers, r/SideProject.

## Reply to a launch post (free sample)
> Congrats on shipping! I made a quick launch video from your site for fun — want it? Free, no strings. [attach first frame or 3-second clip]

## DM after they say yes
> Here it is: [link]. Feel free to post it. If you'd ever want vertical/square cuts for TikTok/Reels or a custom tone, I do these for $29–79: [link]

## Cold DM (no free sample)
> Saw [product] — the "[their best line]" line is great. I make 20-second launch videos straight from the product's code/UI (example: [link]). $29, 48h. Want one for your launch?

> 3 examples 👇 [videos]
